# Brag Plan: ChopDrop

## What is this app?
ChopDrop is a batch food ordering platform for Lagos offices — pick from local restaurants, order lunch with your team, delivered to your desk by noon. Real Nigerian food, real scarcity clocks, real community ordering.

## The angle
"Your office lunch, sorted." Not another delivery app — this is communal food for work. The hook is the problem everyone in a Lagos office knows: "Who's ordering lunch?" becomes an entire group chat. ChopDrop kills that conversation with one tap. Show the flow as if a real person is ordering right now.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Big text slam: **"Lunch sorted."** on warm cream background. Immediate, confident, Nigerian English. Then the ChopDrop logo drops in with the coral teardrop smile.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Search moment:** "What's for lunch today?" typed in a black search pill, surrounded by large illustrated food icons orbiting tight. Camera zooms in while typing, cursor clicks search, then pulls back out.
- **Restaurant pick:** Dark card morphs in from the search bar. "Pick your restaurant" header. Three Lagos restaurants (Ajisafe Kitchen, The Place, Mama T's Kitchen) stagger in. Yellow highlight scans down. Card zooms right, "Top restaurants in Lagos" appears left.
- **Cart + price:** White cart card — Pepper Rice ₦2,500, Jollof Rice + Chicken ₦3,800. Green "PROCEED TO CHECKOUT" button. Real prices, real food.
- **Delivery promise:** "Victoria Island Office" typed into address bar with scattered pins. "Hot lunch, delivered to your desk" + "Arrives by 12:00 PM · 4.2 km" tag.

## Outro / punchline
Dark green (#0F4030) background. ChopDrop logo drops with bounce. "ChopDrop" in coral, defocus-to-focus. "Order your office lunch" tagline. URL button. @chopdrop.ng handle. Hold 2s. The confidence IS the punchline — no joke needed.

## User flow worth showing
Browse menu (search + icons) → Pick restaurant (card with highlight scan) → Add to cart (Pepper Rice + Jollof) → Enter office location → Lunch delivered by noon.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: premium Lagos food-tech launch — warm, confident, communal
- Interpretation: Clean card reveals, smooth wipes between scenes. Typography is bold Manrope, not decorative. Motion is spring-eased, fast entries with adequate holds. No bounce-house energy — tactile and precise, like the Glovo Kenya promo style.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

- Secondary: #0F4030 (deep forest green)

Your office lunch, sorted. ChopDrop — order from Lagos's best restaurants, delivered to your desk by noon.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm upbeat bed, professional motion accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (110 BPM, warm afrobeats-inflected)
- Music treatment: start at 0s, full presence, fade out at 17s over 1s
- Music cue guidance: Vol 12 preset at 110 BPM. Strong cues at 8.74s (cart reveal), 13.11s (delivery text), 17.47s (near outro hold). Beat grid for sequential restaurant row reveals at 5.34s, 6.00s, 6.56s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music energy drives the yellow blur circle opacity pulse
- SFX posture: moderate; whoosh on transitions, pop on logo/card reveals, soft tick on typing
- Audio-coupled moments: search text typing with key ticks, restaurant row arrivals on beats, checkout button pop, pin drops with soft thuds
- Restraint rule: no whoosh spam, no constant ticking. SFX accent the big moments only.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook + Logo — 3.0s
Big bold "Lunch sorted." text slams onto warm cream background. Yellow blur circle at bottom pulses. Text scale-throughs out. ChopDrop coral teardrop icon drops in with back-out ease. Glove hand drags icon left, revealing "ChopDrop" typed letter-by-letter. Ghost trails in coral. Hand flicks out. Logo settles with wobble.
Sequential/interaction: yes — letters type one by one, hand drags icon
Audio intent: confident energy, warm opening
Audio-coupled idea: logo pop on drop-in, subtle key ticks on letter typing
Music: warm start, builds
Transition mood: clean scale-through → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Search + Browse — 2.5s
Black search pill scales in. "What's for lunch today?" types character by character with blinking cursor. 7 large food icons (jollof, suya, burger, pepper soup, moi moi, pounded yam, scooter) orbit tight around bar. Camera zooms in 2.2x during typing — icons zoom with it. Cursor clicks search icon. Camera zooms back out.
Sequential/interaction: yes — text types, camera zooms in then out, cursor clicks
Audio intent: building energy, typing rhythm
Audio-coupled idea: key ticks on typing, soft pop on cursor click
Transition mood: smooth morph → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Pick Restaurant — 3.0s
Search bar morphs into dark card (pill → rectangle, radius 100→24, black→dark). "Pick your restaurant" in amber. Three restaurant rows stagger in on beat grid: Ajisafe Kitchen (coral), The Place (green), Mama T's Kitchen (purple). Yellow highlight scans down through rows. "ORDER NOW" button pops. Card zooms right, "Top restaurants in Lagos" text slides in left.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rows arrive one by one, highlight scans down, card zooms right
Audio intent: rhythmic reveals, satisfying card sounds
Audio-coupled idea: card arrival on beats at ~5.34, 6.00, 6.56
Transition mood: diagonal wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Cart — 2.0s
White cart card materializes. Two items: Pepper Rice ₦2,500 (Ajisafe Kitchen, coral label, food emoji), Jollof Rice + Chicken ₦3,800 (The Place, green label). Green "PROCEED TO CHECKOUT" button pops from bottom. Cursor clicks checkout.
Sequential/interaction: yes — items stagger in fast, button pops
Audio intent: satisfaction, completion
Audio-coupled idea: pop on button appearance
Transition mood: clean fade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Delivery — 2.5s
Address bar appears: "Victoria Island Office" types in. "Confirm Office" button. 5 red SVG teardrop pins drop with elastic bounce, morph to orange. Pins and bar fade. "Hot lunch, delivered to your desk" slides in bold. "Arrives by 12:00 PM · 4.2 km" tag bounces in.
Sequential/interaction: yes — address types, pins drop, delivery text reveals
Audio intent: arrival, warm confidence
Audio-coupled idea: soft thud on pin drops, tag pop on bounce-in
Transition mood: yellow blur fills screen → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 5.0s
Yellow blur circle scales to fill screen then recedes. Dark green #19342d background. ChopDrop coral icon drops with back-out bounce. "ChopDrop" in coral 72px, defocus-to-focus reveal. "Order your office lunch" in amber. URL button "chopdrop-app.vercel.app" scales in. "@chopdrop.ng" handle fades in. Hold 2s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — each element staggers in
Audio intent: warm resolution, confidence
Audio-coupled idea: logo pop on drop, music fades at 17s
Transition mood: final hold
