## 1. PURPOSE

This Agreement establishes the terms of partnership between ChopDrop and the Restaurant for the provision of group food delivery services to office workers in Ikeja, Lagos.

## 2. HOW IT WORKS

2.1. ChopDrop operates a platform where office workers individually select and prepay for meals from the Restaurant's menu.

2.2. ChopDrop collects orders before a daily cutoff time (currently 10:30 AM) and submits a consolidated batch order to the Restaurant.

2.3. The Restaurant prepares the meals as ordered.

2.4. ChopDrop arranges delivery of all meals to the designated office location(s) by the agreed delivery time (currently 12:00 PM — 1:30 PM).

## 3. RESTAURANT OBLIGATIONS

3.1. Provide an up-to-date menu with accurate pricing to ChopDrop.

3.2. Prepare all confirmed orders to the standard quality expected by customers.

3.3. Have orders ready for pickup by the agreed time each day.

3.4. Notify ChopDrop immediately if any menu item becomes unavailable.

3.5. Maintain all necessary food handling and hygiene standards required by law.

## 4. CHOPDROP OBLIGATIONS

4.1. Collect payments from customers on behalf of the Restaurant via the ChopDrop platform.

4.2. Submit consolidated daily orders to the Restaurant before the cutoff time.

4.3. Arrange and pay for delivery from the Restaurant to the office location(s).

4.4. Remit payment to the Restaurant for all fulfilled orders within [7 / 14] business days of delivery.

4.5. Handle customer service enquiries related to the platform and delivery.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1. Menu prices displayed on ChopDrop are set by the Restaurant.

5.2. ChopDrop charges a service fee to customers, which is separate from the Restaurant's menu prices.

5.3. The Restaurant receives the full menu price for each item ordered, less any agreed commission of ____% (if applicable).

5.4. Payments are made via bank transfer to the Restaurant's designated account.

## 6. DELIVERY

6.1. ChopDrop provides delivery at no cost to the Restaurant.

6.2. Delivery is currently limited to Ikeja and surrounding areas (Allen Avenue, Opebi, Computer Village, Alausa, Toyin Street).

## 7. DURATION AND TERMINATION

7.1. This Agreement is effective from the date signed and continues until terminated by either party.

7.2. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice.

7.3. Outstanding payments must be settled within 7 days of termination.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1. Both parties agree to act in good faith and maintain professional standards.

8.2. This Agreement may be amended by mutual written consent.

8.3. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
