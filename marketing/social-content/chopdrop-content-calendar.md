# ChopDrop Content Calendar — Day 0 to Day 60

**Platforms:** Instagram (Reels + Carousels + Stories), TikTok, X (Twitter), WhatsApp Status
**Posting cadence:** 5x/week (Mon–Fri, because our audience is at work)
**Best posting times:** 8:30–9:00 AM (morning scroll), 12:00–12:30 PM (lunch decision time), 6:00–6:30 PM (commute scroll)
**Content pillars:** Pain (relatability), Product (education), Proof (social proof), Culture (office vibes), Promo (conversion)

---

## THE STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Days 0–14 — "WE SEE YOU" (Pain + Awareness)
Build the audience by being the most relatable food content page for Lagos office workers. Zero selling. Pure "this is literally my office" energy. Every piece of content should be screenshot-and-forward worthy.

### Phase 2: Days 15–30 — "THERE'S A BETTER WAY" (Product Introduction)
Start weaving in ChopDrop as the solution. Subtle at first, then direct. The audience already trusts us because Phase 1 made them feel seen.

### Phase 3: Days 31–45 — "JOIN THE MOVEMENT" (Social Proof + Conversion)
Testimonials, office spotlights, user content, and hard CTAs. People have seen the pain, seen the product — now show them real offices using it.

### Phase 4: Days 46–60 — "THE CULTURE" (Community + Retention)
ChopDrop becomes a lifestyle brand for Lagos office culture. Memes, office food rankings, ChopDropper spotlights, expansion teases.

## RULES

1. **Never post on weekends.** Our audience is office workers. They don't think about office lunch on Saturday.
2. **Pain content never dies.** Even at Day 60, 40% of content should be relatability/pain. It's what drives shares.
3. **Every carousel should work as screenshots.** People forward individual slides on WhatsApp. Design for that.
4. **Never use "seamless", "revolutionary", or "game-changer."** Talk like a person, not a pitch deck.
5. **Respond to every comment and DM within 2 hours during work hours.** This is a community, not a billboard.
6. **When in doubt, make them laugh.** Funny > polished. Shareable > beautiful. A meme that gets forwarded to 15 office groups beats a perfectly designed post that gets 30 likes.
