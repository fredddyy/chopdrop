**Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (1080x1080px) or 4:5 (1080x1350px) — go 4:5 for more real estate

## DESIGN TIPS

1. **Every slide should make sense on its own** — people screenshot individual slides. Slide 5 (₦0 delivery) and Slide 7 (comparison) will get screenshotted the most.

2. **Swipe cues on every slide** — small arrow (→) or "swipe" text in bottom-right. People need to be told.

3. **Consistent margins** — 80px padding on all sides minimum. Instagram crops tight on some devices.

4. **Text size hierarchy:**
   - Headlines: 48-64pt Manrope 800
   - Subheads: 28-32pt DM Sans 600
   - Body: 20-24pt DM Sans 400
   - Fine print: 16pt DM Sans 400, 60% opacity

5. **The first slide is everything.** If it doesn't stop the scroll in the feed (where only slide 1 is visible), the other 9 slides don't matter. Make "STRESSING" pop visually — underline it, make it bigger than everything else, add a subtle red glow behind it.

6. **Save a version without the phone mockup on Slide 4** — some people may want to see the actual app larger. You can also use a screen recording as a video slide (Instagram carousels allow mixing images and video).
