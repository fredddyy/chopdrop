# ChopDrop Launch Video Script

**Format:** Vertical (9:16) for Instagram Reels / TikTok / WhatsApp Status
**Duration:** 45–55 seconds
**Tone:** Relatable Lagos office humor → sleek product reveal → emotional payoff
**Music:** Afrobeats lo-fi instrumental (think Sarz-type beat, muted), builds energy at the reveal

---

## SCENE 1: THE CHAOS (0:00 – 0:12)

**[VISUAL: Real office environment. Fluorescent lights. Desks. The vibe is "2:47 PM on a Tuesday in Ikeja."]**

**[TIGHT SHOT: A phone screen. WhatsApp group chat named "Floor 3 Lunch 🍽️" — messages flying:]**

> "Bros what are we eating today"
> "Who is collecting money?"
> "I said RICE not beans"
> "Abeg who ordered amala for me I didn't ask for amala"
> "The food person is not picking up"
> "I'm hungry oh"
> "Me I've transferred my own"
> "Transfer to who??"

**[CUT TO: Close-up of a guy at his desk, rubbing his temples with his eyes closed. The universal Lagos "I'm done" face.]**

**VOICEOVER (male, calm, clean Nigerian accent — not overly formal, not pidgin-heavy):**

> "Every single day... the same story."

**[CUT TO: Another colleague standing in the corridor holding cash, looking confused, checking her phone]**

**VOICEOVER:**

> "Somebody is collecting money. Somebody's order is wrong. Somebody is still waiting."

**[CUT TO: Empty food pack on a desk. Wrong order. The camera lingers on it like a crime scene.]**

**[BEAT — silence — then:]**

**[TEXT ON SCREEN — white on black, Manrope 800:]**

### What if lunch... just worked?

---

## SCENE 2: THE SHIFT (0:12 – 0:18)

**[VISUAL: The energy changes. Camera moves smoothly. Warmer light. A hand picks up a phone. The ChopDrop app opens.]**

**[PRODUCT SHOT: The menu page loads — the real app. Lunch combos visible. Jollof Rice & Beef Combo. ₦1,600. The "Save ₦100" badge. The + button.]**

**VOICEOVER:**

> "ChopDrop. Everyone picks their own meal. Everyone pays on their phone."

**[QUICK CUT: Thumb taps the + button. The coral counter appears: 1. Another tap: 2. Smooth, satisfying.]**

**[CUT TO: The checkout screen. Receipt-style layout. Subtotal, service fee, total. Clean.]**

**VOICEOVER:**

> "No cash. No chasing. No wrong orders."

---

## SCENE 3: THE MAGIC (0:18 – 0:30)

**[VISUAL: Split screen — 4 phones, 4 different people in the same office. All ordering on ChopDrop at the same time. Each screen shows a different meal.]**

**[TEXT ON SCREEN — animated, one word at a time:]**

### Pick. Pay. Done.

**[CUT TO: Kitchen shot — a cook preparing multiple labelled containers. Quick, efficient. (Can use stock footage or shoot at Ajisafe Pepper Rice.)]**

**VOICEOVER:**

> "The kitchen sees exactly what's ordered. Cooks exactly what's needed."

**[CUT TO: A delivery rider arriving at an office building. One bag. Multiple meals inside.]**

**[CUT TO: The ChopDropper — a smiling office assistant at the reception, handing out labelled food packs. People collecting with their phones showing order codes.]**

**VOICEOVER:**

> "One delivery. Zero hassle. Your ChopDropper handles the rest."

**[CUT TO: Someone at their desk, opening their food. Steam rises. It's exactly what they ordered. They look satisfied. Maybe a small smile.]**

## SCENE 4: THE LINE (0:30 – 0:38)

**[VISUAL: The same guy from Scene 1 — the one rubbing his temples. But now he's leaned back in his chair, eating jollof, looking at peace.]**

**[Camera slowly pushes in on him.]**

**VOICEOVER (slower, warmer):**

**[Beat.]**

**[He glances at camera. Almost smiles. Looks back at his food.]**

**[TEXT — amber #F5C565 button style:]**

### Start ordering → chopdrop-app.vercel.app

**[Small text below:]**

No app download needed. Works on any phone.

**[FINAL FRAME: Logo + @chopdrop.ng + WhatsApp icon]**

## VOICEOVER NOTES

- **Voice:** Male or female, 25–35, educated Lagos accent. Not radio-presenter formal. Not full pidgin. The voice of someone you'd trust to organize lunch for the floor — competent, warm, slightly amused.
- **Pace:** Conversational in Scene 1 (matching the chaos). Slows down and gets warmer from Scene 3 onwards. The "Work is stressful. Food shouldn't be." line should land like a hug.
- **DO NOT:** Use "game-changer", "revolutionary", "seamless experience", or any startup buzzwords. This is food for office workers, not a Series A pitch.

---

## MUSIC DIRECTION

- **0:00–0:12 (Chaos):** Slightly anxious, repetitive loop. Think muted percussion + phone notification sounds layered in. Low energy.
- **0:12–0:18 (Shift):** The beat drops — clean, confident Afrobeats instrumental. Not hype, just smooth. Energy lifts.
- **0:18–0:38 (Magic + Line):** Beat continues, slightly fuller. Warm pads underneath. Builds emotion.
- **0:38–0:48 (Brand):** Music reduces to a single sustained chord or soft piano note. Space. Confidence. The logo lands in near-silence.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### What you can shoot yourself:
- WhatsApp chat (create in real WhatsApp or use a chat generator)
- Screen recordings of the real ChopDrop app (use your phone)
- Office shots (any Lagos office with decent lighting)
- The "guy rubbing temples" and "eating at desk" — any friend/colleague

### What you might need:
- Kitchen B-roll (ask Ajisafe Pepper Rice if you can film for 15 minutes)
- A rider shot (can be staged with a friend + a delivery bag)
- Basic motion graphics for text cards (CapCut Desktop handles all of this)

### Tools:
- **Editing:** CapCut Desktop (free, handles everything in this script)
- **Screen recording:** iOS built-in or Android screen recorder
- **Text animation:** CapCut's built-in text templates
- **Music:** CapCut royalty-free library or Epidemic Sound

### Aspect ratios:
- **Primary:** 9:16 vertical (Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status)
- **Secondary:** 1:1 square (Instagram feed, WhatsApp broadcast)
- **Tertiary:** 16:9 landscape (YouTube, website embed, LinkedIn)
