# Brag Plan: NEXT_GEN_TECH — ChopDrop

## What is this app?
ChopDrop kills the "who's ordering lunch?" group chat. Everyone picks their meal, pays on their phone, and one delivery hits the office at noon. ₦0 delivery. 20-30 meals. One drop.

## The angle
Series A startup energy. This isn't a food app — this is infrastructure. This is the lunch stack for Lagos offices. Treat it like a product launch keynote compressed into 20 seconds. Every stat hits like a funding round metric. Every transition is a flex.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Giant text slam: **"LUNCH IS BROKEN."** — white on black. Hard cut. Instant tension. Then: "We fixed it." in coral.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The menu reveal:** App screen showing restaurants, jollof, prices in Naira. "Everyone picks. Everyone pays."
- **The confirmation slam:** Order confirmed. Code CD 028. "by 12:00 PM" in giant type. "₦0 delivery" as a stat hit.
- **The scale flex:** "20-30 meals. One drop." — stats animate in like a funding deck.

## Outro / punchline
ChopDrop logo. "Your office lunch. Disrupted." Beat. URL. Handle.

## User flow worth showing
Browse restaurants (Ajisafe Kitchen, The Place) → Pick meal (Smoky jollof ₦3,800) → Confirmed (code CD 028, by 12:00 PM) → Delivered ₦0.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: Series A startup energy, hyper-intense, pixel-perfect transitions, elastic overshoot scaling, high-velocity pacing
- Interpretation: rapid cuts (some scenes under 2s), text slams in with overshoot, stats hit hard, everything feels like a product launch keynote at 4x speed. Confidence borders on absurd.
