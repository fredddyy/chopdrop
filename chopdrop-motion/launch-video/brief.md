# Brief: ChopDrop Launch Video

## What it is for
A launch promo video for sharing on Instagram Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status, and X. Introduces ChopDrop to Lagos office workers with a "cheat code" angle — deadpan, confident, facts-driven.

## Format
- Primary: 9:16 (1080 x 1920) for Reels/TikTok/Stories

## Duration
21 seconds

## The one sentence
Office workers in Lagos are bleeding money on delivery fees and ChopDrop deletes that expense with free batch delivery.

## Script (voice-over)
Scene 1 (0-3s): "Bro. You know how much you spend on delivery every month? Thirty-two thousand naira. Just delivery."
Scene 2 (3-9s): "So we stopped doing that. Everyone in the office picks their food on ChopDrop... breakfast, lunch, whatever. Pays on their phone."
Scene 3 (9-15s): "Then everything arrives together. One drop. Twelve-thirty. Zero delivery fee."
Scene 4 (15-18s): "You just show your code, collect your food. Done."
Scene 5 (18-21s): [no voice — text punchline]

## Voice-over file
assets/voiceover.mp3 (Bonnie, ElevenLabs, 22.9s)

## Scenes

### Scene 1 — "The Tax" (0-3s)
Dark charcoal (#0A0A0A) with subtle grain. Bold white Manrope text: "₦32,000 every month." holds, then "Just delivery fees." fades in below. Gut-punch math.

### Scene 2 — "The App" (3-9s)
Real screenshot of the ChopDrop menu page inside a phone frame. Shows the breakfast tab, green hero card, food photos, restaurants. Mid-scene: simulated tab switch to Lunch.

### Scene 3 — "The Free Drop" (9-15s)
Checkout screen screenshot. Office Batch Drop selected with "FREE" highlighted. The zero delivery fee is the hero moment.

### Scene 4 — "The Code" (15-18s)
Confirmation screen. Collection code CD-B5AY drops in with spring animation. "You're all set!"

### Scene 5 — "The Flex" (18-21s)
Dark background. Three text lines appear sequentially:
"Some people in your office"
"deleted that expense last week."
"Nobody is talking." (in coral)
Then ChopDrop logo fades in. "Live in Ikeja."

## Sound
- Music: warm lo-fi with Afrobeats undertone, 85-95 BPM, understated. Enters low, carries through, fades to silence under Scene 5.
- SFX: sparse — bass thud on "₦32,000", UI tap on tab switch, clean chime on confirmation code, then silence for the punchline.
