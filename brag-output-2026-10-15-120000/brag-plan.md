# Brag Plan: ChopDrop

## What is this app?
ChopDrop eliminates the ₦32,000/month delivery tax that every Lagos office worker pays without thinking about it — by batching everyone's orders into one free drop.

## The angle
Not about food. Not about restaurants. This is about money you're losing every month to a system nobody questions. ₦800 morning, ₦800 afternoon, ₦1,600/day, ₦32,000/month — just to eat at your own desk. Some people in your office figured out how to delete that line item entirely. They didn't tell you.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
White screen. Large bold text: **"₦32,000 a month."** Holds. Then below: **"Just to eat at your desk."** — the viewer does the math in their head and feels the sting.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The math: ₦800 × 2 × 20 = ₦32,000/month — spelled out so it hits
- The app showing "Office Batch Drop — FREE" at checkout — the delete button for that expense
- Order confirmed with collection code — proof this is real, not a pitch

## Outro / punchline
**"Some people in your office deleted that expense last week. Nobody said a word."** Beat. Then: **"ChopDrop. Ikeja."** Logo. Done.

## User flow worth showing
Open app → see lunch drop with countdown → add food → checkout shows FREE delivery → confirmed with code

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan (overridden)
- Creative direction: CMO-grade growth copy. Not a food app ad. A financial wake-up call disguised as a screen recording. The ego play is that you found the cheat code to delete a monthly expense. Minimalist, raw, Apple-bold text screens between high-fidelity UI captures.
- Interpretation: Slow, confident. Let the numbers sit. No rush. The math does the selling. Plain white screens with heavy black type. UI captures are clean, untouched — not mocked up.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #ffffff (text screens), #fafaf8 (app screens)
- Accent: #f05f4b (coral — only in app UI, never on text screens)
- Text: #1a1a1a (charcoal, Manrope Bold, tight tracking)
- Display font: Manrope
- Body font: DM Sans
- Strongest visual element: the checkout screen showing FREE delivery, the order confirmation with collection code

## Share copy (draft)
₦800 morning. ₦800 afternoon. ₦32,000 a month just to eat at your desk. Some people in your office deleted that expense. Nobody said a word. ChopDrop is live in Ikeja.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, barely there
- Music: low-volume lo-fi afrobeats instrumental, smooth and steady — background texture only
- Music treatment: fades in at scene 1, stays very low, no lift, fades to near-silence at the punchline
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time; target strong cue at ~5s (the math reveal), ~16s (order confirmed)
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: minimal; one tap on the + button, one chime on confirmation. That's it.
- Audio-coupled moments: tap sound (scene 3), confirmation chime (scene 4)
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no transitions sounds. The silence between scenes is intentional.

## Narration script (--voice enabled)

Scene 1: "Thirty-two thousand naira. Every month. Just delivery fees."
Scene 2: "Eight hundred in the morning. Eight hundred in the afternoon. Twenty working days."
Scene 3: (silence — the UI shows FREE)
Scene 4: "Done. Food's coming by twelve."
Scene 5: (silence — the punchline text carries itself)

Voice direction: Nigerian accent. Conversational, dry, like doing mental math out loud to yourself. Not selling. Realizing.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Number — 4s
White screen. Large Manrope Bold text centered:
**"₦32,000 a month."**
Appears instantly. Holds 2s.
Then smaller line fades in below:
**"Just to eat at your desk."**
Holds 1.5s.
Narration: "Thirty-two thousand naira. Every month. Just delivery fees."
Sequential/interaction: yes — subtitle appears after headline
Audio intent: near-silence, the number speaks
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Math — 4s
White screen. Three lines appear one by one, left-aligned, stacked:
**₦800 morning**
**₦800 afternoon**
**₦1,600 / day × 20 days**
Each line appears with a 0.8s gap. The third line is bold coral (#f05f4b).
Then a thin line and below it: **= ₦32,000 / month** in large type.
Narration: "Eight hundred in the morning. Eight hundred in the afternoon. Twenty working days."
Sequential/interaction: yes — lines appear one by one, total appears last
Audio intent: quiet accumulation, the music bed is barely audible
Audio-coupled idea: none — the pacing of the lines IS the rhythm
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Delete Button — 5s
Full-screen capture of ChopDrop checkout. "Office Batch Drop" selected with green checkmark showing **FREE**. Below it: "First order — delivery is free! No code needed." in green text. The total shows the food price only — no delivery fee. A simulated thumb scrolls slightly to reveal the full card.
Narration: (silence — let the FREE speak)
Sequential/interaction: yes — slight scroll to reveal the full checkout
Audio intent: music present but very low, the visual contrast (₦32K vs FREE) is the moment
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Proof — 3.5s
Order confirmation screen. Dark green checkmark. "You're all set!" Collection code **CD-B5AY** in large bold type. Below: "Cooked fresh, arrives by 12 PM."
Narration: "Done. Food's coming by twelve."
Sequential/interaction: yes — checkmark scales in
Audio intent: soft resolution, one quiet chime
Audio-coupled idea: chime on checkmark
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The Punchline — 6.5s
White screen. Two lines, Manrope Bold, centered:
**"Some people in your office deleted that expense last week."**
Holds 2.5s.
Second line appears:
**"Nobody said a word."**
Holds 2s.
ChopDrop logo (teardrop with smile) fades in small, center-bottom. Below it in small grey: **"Ikeja"**
Holds 1.5s.
Narration: (silence)
Sequential/interaction: yes — second line after first, logo fades in last
Audio intent: music fades to nothing. The silence after "Nobody said a word" is the whole point.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: fade to black

**Music mood for this video:** understated lo-fi afrobeats, barely there, texture not feature
**Audio summary:** Music is wallpaper. Voice carries scenes 1, 2, 4 only — dry, conversational, doing math. Silence on scenes 3 and 5 lets the UI and the punchline do the work. Two SFX total. The restraint is the brand.
