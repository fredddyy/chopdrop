# Brag Plan: ChopDrop

## What is this app?
ChopDrop batches individual food orders from an entire office into one free delivery — everyone picks their own meal, pays on their phone, food arrives together at 12:30. Live in Ikeja, Lagos.

## The angle
"The Delivery Tax Cheat Code." Every office worker in Lagos bleeds ₦32,000/month on individual breakfast + lunch delivery fees without realizing it. ChopDrop deletes that line item. The video is a sharp, lowkey flex from someone who already found the hack — not a pitch, a confession.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Bold white text punches in on a dark textured background (not sterile white — dark charcoal with a subtle grain): "₦32,000 every month." Beat. "Just delivery fees." This is the gut-punch math that makes office workers do the mental calculation on their own spending.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real ChopDrop menu screen inside a phone frame — breakfast/lunch tabs, real food photos, flip-clock timer ticking. The phone is alive, not a screenshot.
- The checkout screen: "Office Batch Drop" with the green checkmark and bold "FREE" — while Express shows ₦1,200 crossed out. The ₦0 delivery is the hero moment.
- The confirmation code CD-B5AY dropping in like a premium digital ticket. Food arrives at 12:30. Done.

## Outro / punchline
Apple-style centered text on dark background: "Some people in your office deleted that expense last week." Beat. "Nobody is talking." Beat. ChopDrop logo + "Live in Ikeja."

## User flow worth showing
1. Menu page with Breakfast tab active → real food cards (Ajisafe peppered rice photo) → timer counting down
2. Checkout with Office Batch Drop selected → FREE highlighted green → food in cart with real image
3. Confirmation: "You're all set!" → collection code CD-B5AY → "Food arrives at 12:30"

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan
- Creative direction: "sharp Lagos tech worker chatting over lunch, lowkey flexing a cheat code, casual Nigerian English, zero marketing fluff, conversational"
- Interpretation: Slow, confident pacing. No rush. Every beat lands with space around it. The humor is dry — the absurdity is that ₦32k is just... gone. Voice-over carries the personality; visuals are clean and premium. No exclamation marks, no hype — just facts delivered with a knowing smirk.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0a0a (near-black for text scenes), #ffffff (for app UI scenes)
- Accent: #f05f4b (coral — CTAs, deals, brand energy)
- Text: #ffffff on dark, #1a1a1a on light
- Display font: Manrope (bold, tight tracking)
- Body font: DM Sans
- Strongest visual element: The dark green hero card with flip-clock countdown + the food photo restaurant cards

## Share copy (draft)
"Your office is spending ₦32,000/month on delivery fees. Some buildings in Ikeja deleted that expense last week. Nobody is talking. chopdrop-app.vercel.app"

## Audio direction
- Role: warm minimal bed — voice carries, music is texture
- Music: lo-fi Afrobeats-inflected warm pad, 85-95 BPM, understated groove. Not hype — confident.
- Music treatment: fade in from 0s at 40% volume, sits under voice the whole time, slight swell at the confirmation reveal (scene 4), fade out over final 2s
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time. Target major visual moments at ~3s (app reveal), ~12s (₦0 delivery hit), ~18s (logo). Beat-grid windows for the checkout card reveal.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; hero card and confirmation code may pulse gently with bass
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: UI tap on tab switch, soft whoosh on checkout card selection, clean chime on confirmation code reveal, subtle tick on countdown digits
- Restraint rule: no bass drops, no hype transitions, no whoosh-swoosh on every element. The deadpan delivery IS the energy. SFX are surgical accents, not wallpaper.

## Voice-over
- Enabled: yes
- Tone: casual Nigerian English, like explaining something to a colleague at lunch. Not presenter voice — real person voice. Unhurried. Dry.
- Script:

Scene 1 (0-3s): "Bro. You know how much you spend on delivery every month? Thirty-two thousand naira. Just delivery."

Scene 2 (3-9s): "So we stopped doing that. Everyone in the office picks their food on ChopDrop... breakfast, lunch, whatever. Pays on their phone."

Scene 3 (9-15s): "Then everything arrives together. One drop. Twelve-thirty. Zero delivery fee."

Scene 4 (15-18s): "You just show your code, collect your food. Done."

Scene 5 (18-21s): [no voice — just the text punchline landing with weight]

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — "The Tax" — 3s
Dark charcoal background (#0a0a0a) with subtle film grain texture. Bold white Manrope text punches in: "₦32,000 every month." Hold 1.2s. Second line fades in below: "Just delivery fees." The text is large, centered, unapologetic. This is not inside a phone — it's a raw statement.
Sequential/interaction: Two-line staggered text reveal (line 1 punches, line 2 fades in 0.8s later)
Audio intent: Quiet tension. Music bed starts very low.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle bass thud on "₦32,000" text punch
Voice-over: "Bro. You know how much you spend on delivery every month? Thirty-two thousand naira. Just delivery."
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — "The App" — 6s
Full-screen realistic iPhone frame. Inside it: the actual ChopDrop menu page. Breakfast tab is active (dark pill). The green hero card shows "Breakfast Drop" with flip-clock digits counting. Below: real food photo restaurant cards (Ajisafe peppered rice image). Mid-scene: simulated tap switches to Lunch tab — the hero card updates to "Lunch Drop", restaurant cards cross-fade. The phone is alive, not a still.
Sequential/interaction: Yes — tab switch interaction simulated at 3s mark. Cards cross-fade on tab change.
Audio intent: Warm, confident. Music bed rises slightly. The app feels premium and real.
Audio-coupled idea: Soft UI tap sound on tab switch. Subtle card-in whoosh on restaurant card appearance.
Voice-over: "So we stopped doing that. Everyone in the office picks their food on ChopDrop... breakfast, lunch, whatever. Pays on their phone."
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — "The Free Drop" — 6s
Same iPhone frame, now showing the checkout screen. Cart has "Jollof Rice & Beef Combo" with the Ajisafe food photo, quantity 2, ₦1,600. Below: the Delivery section. "Office Batch Drop" is selected with green border, green checkmark, bold "FREE". Express Delivery below shows ₦1,200 (unselected, muted). The ₦0 delivery line is the visual hero — maybe a subtle green glow pulse on "FREE". Summary at bottom: Total ₦1,760. Service fee (5%) ₦88.
Sequential/interaction: Yes — simulate the Office Batch Drop card getting tapped/selected. The green border and checkmark animate in. "FREE" scales up briefly.
Audio intent: Satisfaction. The money-saving moment. Music sits warmly.
Audio-coupled idea: Clean UI selection sound on the batch drop card tap. Soft chime accent on "FREE" appearing.
Voice-over: "Then everything arrives together. One drop. Twelve-thirty. Zero delivery fee."
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — "The Code" — 3s
iPhone frame showing the confirmation screen. Dark green checkmark at top. "You're all set!" Bold collection code: CD-B5AY in large Manrope 800 weight. Below: "Show this to your captain at collection." The code is the star — rendered like a premium digital pass. Below it: "What happens next" steps visible but secondary.
Sequential/interaction: The code scales in with a spring animation. Checkmark appears first, then code drops in.
Audio intent: Resolution. Clean. The deal is done.
Audio-coupled idea: Premium chime on code reveal. Single clean tone.
Voice-over: "You just show your code, collect your food. Done."
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — "The Flex" — 3s
Back to dark background (#0a0a0a). Three lines of centered white text, appearing one at a time with deliberate spacing:
Line 1: "Some people in your office"
Line 2: "deleted that expense last week."
Line 3 (coral #f05f4b): "Nobody is talking."
Hold 1s. Then: ChopDrop logo (teardrop with smile) fades in small and centered. Below it in small muted text: "Live in Ikeja."
Sequential/interaction: Yes — three text lines appear sequentially (0.6s apart). Logo fades in last.
Audio intent: The mic drop. Music fades. Silence after the last word.
Audio-coupled idea: None — silence IS the sound design here. Music fades out under the text.
Voice-over: none — the text speaks for itself
Transition mood: end
